QREX is a QR scanner and creator for Android designed by Brendigo.

• Scan QR codes with the camera or choose an image from the gallery.
• Create QR codes for text, links, Wi-Fi, contacts, email, phone numbers and locations.
• Review scanned links before choosing to open them.
• Keep local history and favorites, with controls to delete saved items.
• Export QR images as PNG and saved QR content as PDF or ZIP.
• Choose light or dark appearance, with offline interface translations in 23 languages.

QREX does not require an account, advertisements or an internet permission. QR creation and scanning operate on your device. Camera permission is requested for live scanning only.

Saved Wi-Fi QR data is protected with Android Keystore where applicable. PDF and ZIP exports are not encrypted: treat exported personal data as sensitive.

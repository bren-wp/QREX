# QREX — Scan. Create. Share.

## Android v1.0.1 · versionCode 101

Stability, accessible compact results, memory-bounded streaming PDF/ZIP exports, source/dead-code audit and Google Play readiness. The Android-only app keeps its offline scanning, encrypted manually saved Wi-Fi history, 23 languages and local Flutter-to-native migration reader.

**Two separate channels:** [GitHub downloads](https://github.com/bren-wp/QREX/releases) use a disposable certificate for directly installable APKs and non-Play AABs. **Google Play requires the independent Play-authorized 1.0.1 AAB** built with the existing verified upload key. The public v1.0.1 APK cannot upgrade a v1.0.0 APK signed with another disposable key. Do not uninstall your existing QREX copy without a safe data export.

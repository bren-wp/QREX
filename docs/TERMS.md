## Using QREX

QREX helps you scan, create, save, and share QR codes. You may use QREX for lawful purposes and only share or export QR codes, credentials, or contact details that you are authorized to disclose.

QR codes can point to external websites and services controlled by third parties. Always check the destination and contents before opening a link, connecting to a Wi-Fi network, making a call, or sharing information. QREX does not verify, endorse, or guarantee the safety of external destinations.

## Exports and backups

The optional PDF export contains readable QR codes you manually saved. If a saved QR code includes a Wi-Fi password, contact details, or other private information, **the exported PDF contains that information and is not encrypted**. Protect the document, choose a trusted destination, and share it only with authorized recipients. The operating system or a storage provider may retain a copy after export. Removing QREX history does not remove previously exported files.

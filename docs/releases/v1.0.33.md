## Android native screen 01 — Splash

Comparing user-provided reference imagery to twelve authentic 1080x1920 emulator captures (CI run 41559633080) revealed the lower bright blue ribbon crosses the QREX wordmark and the emblem halo uses disproportionately heavy bands. This refines the actual Compose screen without inserting a background screenshot:

- Moves the primary lower ribbon and its luminous contour below the wordmark/tagline using revised cubic Bézier control points (starting at 76% and cresting around 70% of available content height); shifts the lower complementary stroke to 86–92%.
- Reduces halo stroke widths from 20/9dp to 10/5dp and adds a crisp 1.5dp cyan edge, retaining the official Q/QR vector.
- Locally formatted full-width footer breaks at em dash into two balanced lines at regular viewport width; compact devices and already multiline translations preserve the original text. Footer bottom spacing changes from 58dp to 40dp at normal heights, 26dp to 20dp for short devices.
- No changes to QR decoding, Create screen data, 23 localizations, encrypted Wi-Fi secrets, on-device processing or application ID.
- Source and JVM tests cover the actual wave paths, geometry and localized line breaks. Compose instrumentation guards against overlap and minimum visible progress width.

# QREX 0.2.0 RC 1 — Android & iOS native preview

**Pre-release • for validation only • not a signed Google Play / Apple App Store package**

A new platform-focused QREX experience built with Kotlin / Jetpack Compose for Android and SwiftUI for iOS, emphasizing private QR scanning and creation.

## What's new

- Improved QR creation, previews, local history and optional Wi-Fi password storage.
- Android CameraX + on-device QR recognition and dedicated image picker.
- Android Keystore encryption of saved Wi-Fi QR values, with fewer expensive encryptions when history changes.
- iPhone camera + photo recognition, on-device QR creation, and Keychain-backed history.
- QR image exporting through native Android/iOS share and file interfaces.
- 23 interface languages, English by default.
- Clear failure messages if a protected storage action cannot complete.
- Stronger legacy Flutter history migration protection and data erasure.
- Verified emulator/simulator screenshot artifacts and expanded automation.

## What these files mean

- `QREX-Android-debug-0.2.0-rc.1.apk` is a **debug-signed, installable QA application** for testers, **not a Play release**; it is not signed with the existing Google Play upload key.
- `QREX-Android-unsigned-0.2.0-rc.1.aab` is an **unsigned Android App Bundle for release-pipeline validation**, not acceptable for uploading to Google Play without the authorized upload key.
- `QREX-iOS-unsigned-0.2.0-rc.1.xcarchive.zip` is an **unsigned device-archive candidate for Xcode review and subsequent authorized signing**, **not an installable IPA**.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt` records asset hashes for verification.
- Included screenshots were captured from running applications in emulators/simulators, not drawn marketing mockups.

## Before a production release

Verify actual user flows and visual design on physical Android and Apple devices, test upgrades from the public Flutter edition, approve Apple signing and TestFlight, apply the **existing** Google Play upload key, and review Data safety / App Store privacy declarations, app permissions and accessibility.

**Existing v0.1.3 remains the latest public stable edition.** This candidate must not replace a signed production application or be represented as an approved store release.

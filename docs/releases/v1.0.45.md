- Repair the post-merge Android native CI regression caused by removing the exact published-release README marker; repair the v1.0.44 screenshot link and document real existing assets.

## Reference accuracy limits

The six references are framed smartphone illustrations rather than unframed emulator PNGs. Their pixel mismatch against actual running-app screenshots cannot be computed directly; 0% visual deviation is **not verified**. The live scanner must always display real CameraX content, not a staged background image.

## Production safety

Use the unchanged Play upload certificate through the separately authorized production signing workflow for Google Play updates. Do not upload the CI-signed public AAB to Play or attempt to replace existing locally encrypted data by reinstalling from another certificate.

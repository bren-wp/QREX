# QREX 0.2.1 RC1 — Scan. Create. Share.

**Testing preview • not an app store release**

Get the refreshed QREX native experience for Android and iPhone: simple QR scanning, one-tap code creation and sharing, private saved codes, and export of a printable PDF collection in ZIP format.

### More polished, safer by design

- An upgraded QR workflow on Android and iPhone, with English as the first-launch language and 22 additional languages including Croatian.
- Settings with live history and appearance controls, Brendigo attribution, and direct access to the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
- User-confirmed PDF/ZIP export containing printable versions of manually saved QR codes. **The ZIP is not encrypted and may include saved Wi-Fi passwords.**
- Protected local QR history, including Android Keystore protection for saved Wi-Fi codes and Keychain-backed iPhone records.
- Android adaptive/themed icons and original QREX graphics; launch-screen and real emulator screenshot testing.

### What's included in this QA release

- Android **debug-signed** installable APK for testers, not signed with your Google Play upload key.
- Android **unsigned** AAB for packaging verification, not suitable for Play Console upload.
- Apple **unsigned** iOS device archive for Xcode signing validation, not an installable IPA.
- Actual Android emulator and iPhone simulator screenshots and SHA-256 checksums.

### What still requires verification

Authorized Android and Apple signing, upgrading existing users without losing history, real-device scanning and gallery behavior, accessibility, screenshot-to-design review, and Google Play / App Store Connect disclosures and approval.

**The last previous legacy Flutter Android release is v0.1.3. QREX native RC1 is a separate testing edition, not an approved store build.**

# QREX 0.2.2 RC1 — Your QR essentials, clearly connected

**Android & iPhone QA preview • not production signed • not an app-store release**

Keep the QREX experience you know: scan QR codes, create and share new ones, store favorites locally and export saved QR codes in a printable PDF inside a ZIP archive.

### What's improved

- **Working privacy and legal links:** Android and iPhone Settings now lead to the QREX Privacy Policy and Terms of Use in the correct repository.
- **A clearer first impression:** the QREX README directs users to authentic QREX release downloads, actual Android and iPhone screenshots, and the publisher's current legal documentation.
- **Consistent build identity:** Android and iOS marketing versions advance together to **0.2.2**, with Android `versionCode 7` and iOS `build 7`.
- **Honest release labelling:** the release pipeline corrects misclassified 0.2.0/0.2.1 release candidates and keeps 0.2.2 RC1 a GitHub pre-release rather than a stable production build.
- **Favorites stay favorites:** the Android and iOS migration paths now preserve the saved status of a QR code even when it appears in both a new record and older Flutter history, or is repeated in a legacy collection. Device-backed Android and iOS regression cases cover the behavior.
- **Regression protection:** automated tests prevent obsolete legal/download links and mismatched build-version metadata from returning.

### QA-only downloads

When all exact-commit GitHub Actions checks succeed, the candidate contains an **Android debug APK**, **unsigned Android AAB**, **unsigned Apple device archive**, actual emulator/simulator screenshots and **SHA-256 checksums**. These are for verification, **not direct Google Play or App Store submission**.

The printable QR archive is **not encrypted**. Exported codes may expose saved Wi-Fi credentials or other private information.

### Before production distribution

Authorized Android Play upload signing and Apple code signing/provisioning, physical-device testing, accessibility review, migration validation and accurate Google Play / App Store Connect disclosures are still required. A passing CI build is not proof of approval by either store.
